Haldia Institute of Technology — Attendance Policy (Sample)

1. Minimum Attendance
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each theory and laboratory course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

2. Condonation
Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation through the Head of Department. A medical certificate is required for absence exceeding three consecutive days.

3. Detention
Attendance below 65% results in detention. The student must repeat the course in a subsequent semester.

4. Leave of Absence
Official leave (sports, NSS, academic events) must be pre-approved by the Dean of Student Affairs and is counted toward attendance.

5. Contact
For attendance queries contact the Academic Section: academics@hit-haldia.edu.in
