FULL ARCHITECTURE

16 Autonomous Intelligence Engines

Not a wrapper around an LLM. DocuLoom combines 16 specialized modules for complete epistemic trust.

⏱

Temporal Reasoning

Answers "what was the rule in 2024?" vs "what is the rule today?" by analyzing document validity dates and tracking supersession chains.

Engine A
✅

Evidence Claim Verification

Decomposes generated answers into atomic claims and verifies each against source chunks. Computes citation coverage and flags hallucinated claims.

Engine B
🧭

Adaptive Retrieval Planning

Classifies queries before search. Routes factual lookups to BM25, semantic questions to dense embeddings, and temporal queries to date filters.

Engine C
⚡

Document Impact Analysis

On every document upload, automatically calculates which other circulars are affected, extracts relationships, and identifies affected stakeholders.

Engine D
🔍

Complete Query Provenance

Every response records retrieval ranks, dense/sparse scores, prompt versions, and chunk snapshots, enabling exact historical query replay.

Engines E + I
💾

Semantic Cache with Fingerprinting

Embeds questions for instant sub-10ms cache hits. Automatically invalidates cached answers when documents or circulars are updated.

Engine F
🩺

Self-Healing Ingestion Quality

Evaluates OCR confidence, text coverage, and empty chunk ratios on upload. Quarantines unreadable scans and offers 1-click reprocess.

Engine G
⚗️

Counterfactual Testing Sandbox

Simulate document edits or deletions in memory to test how retrieval outputs change before pushing policy revisions to students.

Engine H
🔀

Query Decomposition

Breaks complex multi-part questions into independent sub-queries, retrieves evidence for each separately, and synthesizes one coherent answer.

Engine J
⚠️

Contradiction Discovery Radar

Proactively scans the entire corpus for conflicting numbers, rules, and clauses. Surfaces contradictions with severity scores and resolve tools.

Engine K
🕐

Knowledge Expiration Tracker

Tracks expiration dates, sunset clauses, and review schedules. Alerts campus administrators before stale circulars mislead students.

Engine L
📡

Retrieval Drift Detection

Evaluates Recall@K, MRR, and nDCG against a golden question baseline on index changes. Fires alerts when quality drops below threshold.

Engine M
🏅

Document Trust Scoring

Computes an explainable trust score (0.0 to 1.0) for every document based on authority, recency, validity, and OCR quality.

Engine N
📊

Query Difficulty Estimation

Heuristically estimates question difficulty to optimize compute: skips expensive reranking for simple facts, boosts top-k for complex questions.

Engine O
🗺

Knowledge Gap Discovery

Logs all unanswered student questions, clusters them by topic, and provides prioritized recommendations on which circulars need publishing.

Engine P
🛡️

Strict Tenant & Auth Isolation

Complete data segregation by university, college, or department with token authentication and customizable credit limits.
